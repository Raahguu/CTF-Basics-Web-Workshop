Find Your Direction

Here at North Star Solutions, we build applications that help you find north. And east. And west. And south.

Our products are inspired by the timeless elegance of the compass. Some of them function exactly like a compass. Others look like one. A few combine both approaches.

Download Our Compass App

Our Products

  • TrueNorth - a digital compass that points north
  • ForeverForward - a digital compass that always points forward
  • Westward - a digital compass that points west
  • CosmoCompass - a decorative compass for people who enjoy circles
  • Compass Lite - a smaller compass
  • Adventurer's Compass - a digital compass that when opened each time chooses a random direction to point in
  • FreedomCompass - a world first compass with no needle

All apps are free. We do not currently charge for anything. We also do not sell data, subscriptions, or physical compasses.

Our Mission

We believe the world needs more direction. Specifically, cardinal direction.

While many companies chase profits, we remain focused on perfecting the digital compass experience.
